Clients

The companies I work with.

The founders and teams I'm partnered with right now — what they're building, and a little about the people behind them.

NM
Nachi Mehta 3 months
Stackless — AI Platform for Data Teams

Stackless helps large companies wrangle messy, scattered data into something people can actually use. Meaning, they use AI to auto-create dashboards and insights for these businesses. Their secret sauce is they know how to fix and fine-tune the "plumbing" so the AI actually works. Nachi is smart (graduated high school at 15) and had been doing this as a consultant for years before starting the company. He has a small team of 4–6.

AG
Austin Geraci 47 months
WorldTech IT — Cybersecurity & Networking

Austin built WorldTech into a serious IT security and networking company out of Austin, TX, with its own SaaS products layered on top. He's a true builder: he loves the start of things, and he's also a great musician on the side. He's actively working on selling WTIT and starting another business focused on AI hardware.

MF
Madeline Fraser 3 months
Gemist — AI and Website SaaS for the Jewelry Industry

80% of all jewelry is custom designed. Gemist lets jewelry retailers work with clients faster and more easily by using AI to create images and mockups for customers, both in-person and on the web. She also connects jewelers to brands and manufacturers of stones, and is in the middle of raising a round with the largest jewelry insurer. Madeline is a repeat founder. She lives in Ojai and runs a working farm — vineyard, chickens, and all.

ME
Max Eisendrath 52 months
Redflag — AI Content Protection

Redflag listens to the web and can find any piece of content, anywhere, especially on social media and piracy sites. They use their technology to help content creators and content owners — from major sports leagues to YouTube creators — from having their work stolen and reposted online. Max is a sole founder who still codes, still manages his engineering team, and is building partnerships with some of the biggest names in media.

JP
JP 29 months
Storyboard — AI Platform for Trucking Fleets

Storyboard brings AI-powered automation to trucking and fleet dispatch — replacing paperwork and guesswork with tools that keep drivers safer and fleets running smoothly. They've grown steadily and are shooting for a big year. JP is a great founder who's also a big-time movie buff (like me!).

DL
David Lewis 8 months
MoveEV — EV Fleet Charging

MoveEV makes sure businesses that have electric vehicle fleets can repay their employees accurately for charging costs. This is unglamorous but essential infrastructure for the EV transition. David's a local hustler with a small team, trying to bootstrap to profitability. He's a Marin County dad who coaches his daughter's softball team between sales calls.

HA
Henry Abenaim 52 months
Fundingo — SaaS Platform for Lenders

Fundingo builds software for complex lending operators (think a hot tub business that also makes loans). They're growing fast. Henry's a father of five who is genuinely curious about all things.

AC
Avinash Joshi & Ajith 16 months
Cactus — AI for Home Services Companies

A YC company building software for HVAC and home services businesses to help them capture more leads. Their AI answers incoming phone calls, does auto outreach to leads via text and phone calls, and follows up with leads via text, email and phone so the companies can book more jobs. Avinash (CTO) and Ajith (CEO) are college best friends.

K
Kyle 9 months
Wild West Social House — Membership Club & Fashion Rental

A Rent the Runway meets Soho House — a boutique membership in LA that lets people rent high-fashion gear (bags, shoes, clothes) and also attend fun events like poker nights and a Coachella tent. They have celebrities and stylists as customers and are growing nicely, with plans for ecommerce and New York next. Kyle is both a former male model and a huge growth-mindset data nerd.

JH
Jake Hirabayashi 41 months
The Futures App — Youth Sports Software

The Futures App is software (with built-in payments and banking) for youth sports organizations across the country. Growing steadily. Jake, a former pro baseball player (MN Twins!), is CEO of a small 12-person team. They're actively talking with private equity now so they can get access to more capital and start to acquire adjacent businesses into one platform.

SM
Shazil Mehkri 44 months
Sastaticket — Online Travel

The leading online travel booking platform in Pakistan, coming up on its 10th anniversary. Shazil is bringing U.S. tactics to the Pakistani market. Growth is slow but they're profitable, and as the market matures, they should scale accordingly. Shazil loves to watch and train for MMA, and has two daughters and one dog (Bernie Sanders).

PE
Jake Yurek & Evan Anderson Just started
Pearl Edison — Home Electrification Marketplace

College friends from Notre Dame building a marketplace that connects homeowners with vetted contractors for home electrification — heat pumps, hot water, weatherization — partnering with utilities to make it easy. We just started working together in August, so we're still getting to know each other.

JB
Jori Bell 12 months
Hampton — Founder Community

She's the Head of Product for Hampton's core groups — groups of 8 founders that meet monthly. A good product leader (former Amazon and Spotify) and former full-time coach of founders, she's about to leave for maternity leave from October to January.
