What I actually do.

Four problems I help founders solve. Most engagements touch all of them.

M 01 · Milestone

Hit your milestone.

Fundraise, profitability, sale — there's a finish line you're running at, and something's keeping you from it. We find it, work backwards from the goal, and avoid the cliff.

G 02 · GTM

GTM that compounds.

When the curve isn't bending, we pull GTM apart and iterate until it does. Understand the motion you actually have, then build the one you need.

H 03 · Team

Hire A-players.

Your team is your company's DNA. The better the team, the more you can run in parallel — and the faster the company can move. We sharpen the bar and the process behind it.

L 04 · Leverage

Get leverage.

Working harder isn't the answer. You have to lead people, not be the bottleneck on every problem. Which means working on yourself first.
