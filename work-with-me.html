Work with Mike

A small number of leaders. Real relationships. Ongoing.

I work with a limited number of founders and companies at a time — usually in an ongoing advisory and coaching relationship.

By application 3-month minimum SF · Worldwide
01 · How we start

We start with a hike.

Three hours on foot. No deck, no agenda, no laptop — just enough time and distance to actually get to know each other and the company you're trying to build.

I do this with every new CEO I take on. The conversation goes deeper outside than it ever does on Zoom.

I live in Marin. If you're elsewhere, we'll find a trail that works — or do the first hike when you next pass through.

02 · Engagement

What working together looks like.

Cadence
Weekly, biweekly, or custom.

Most engagements settle into a weekly rhythm with a longer working session once a quarter. Cadence flexes around what the moment calls for.

Format
Zoom · walk-and-talk · on-site when it matters.

Default is video. Some clients prefer phone calls on walks. I'll come on-site for offsites, board prep, and high-stakes weeks.

Between sessions
Available over Slack.

I'm in your Slack between sessions. Quick reads on a hard message, a board memo, a decision that can't wait until next Tuesday.

Scope
Know the entire team.

I meet with your team and board to get the context of the business, see the impact of your behavior, and uplevel their process and thinking. Plus — CEOs can be unreliable narrators. 🙂

Offsites
Attend, plan, and lead.

I can attend, help plan, or run offsites so they actually move the company forward instead of becoming the kind of offsite people complain about for weeks after.

Commitment
Three months minimum. Most go longer.

The work compounds. The first few sessions are reconnaissance. The real leverage shows up after.
