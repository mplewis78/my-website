2015 → 2019

Product Lead.

Airbnb · Lux + Airbnb for Work

Four years inside Airbnb during the scaling years — from roughly 400 employees to 9,000. Two years leading product for the Lux business unit, managing 6 PMs across supply growth, host and guest experience, concierge, payments, infra. Before that, first product hire on Airbnb for Work: launched the first product in Aug 2015 and grew the business from $0 to $2B in revenue. 6 PMs · 400 → 9,000 employees · $0 → $2B revenue
