Things I built for myself.

These are all apps I wanted to use throughout my daily life that didn't exist, so I built them. They're side projects, but most are live — click through to try any of them.

MealBuddy.

Log food and meals using just your camera, see your weekly nutrition stats, then chat with AI about your habits and get suggestions.

  • 01Log any meal by pointing your camera.

  • 03Chat with AI about your habits.
